Glossary

The record's conclusion labels and terms in its own definitions, searchable: the 199 entries of the A to Z section of sideband/docs/GLOSSARY.md, each with its source line. The box above the list gives this page's plain words for the terms that come up most.

Plain words for the terms that come up most

Draft model, target model
The small model that proposes tokens and the large model whose output is the answer. In sideband the pair is a small Llama draft (about one billion parameters) with a larger Llama target (about eight billion); amber's pair is a Llama draft of about one billion with a target of about three billion; the regime line of each finding names them exactly.
Verify call
One forward pass of the target over a block of drafted tokens. Tokens per verify call is the record's unit for conversion: how many tokens each check emits.
The stream, telemetry, sideband
What a memory or compute device could observe about the target's forward pass: which weight slots or which experts were used. At S3 no value is seen; S1 needs the activation values to rank its units. The sideband is the narrow channel that would carry it to the drafter.
Harvest, primary and replication
A run of the target alone over the committed prompts that logs the stream and the free summaries at every block start. Each source has a primary harvest and a replication harvest on fresh seeds; a label must hold on both.
Probe, reader
A small classifier trained on the features at a block start to predict a label some positions ahead. The record's registered reader is a small multilayer network; three stronger readers were tried afterwards.
Anchor, offset
The anchor is the last verified position, whose forward produced the stream and the free summaries. The offset is how many tokens ahead the probe is asked to predict; the pinned cells use two.
Proxy baseline, full free baseline
The first reads gave the probe without the stream only the target's bucket summaries. The deciding read gave it everything free: the next token summaries, the top candidate ids and the anchor's hidden state.
Equal dims, the noise twin
To price the cost of simply adding inputs, the probe without the stream gets a block of the same size whose alignment to the text is destroyed. A margin only counts above that twin.
Gate D, materiality floor
D is the accuracy difference between the probe with the stream and the equal dims twin. The floor is the smallest D that was agreed to matter, fixed before the runs.
The bar
For conversion, the gain in tokens per verify call that was agreed to count as a win, fixed before the runs, with the rule that the interval must also clear zero.
Prompt cluster bootstrap
The interval method: resample whole prompts with replacement, recompute the statistic, take the middle 95%. Positions inside one prompt are correlated, so prompts are the unit.
Registration, pinned
In sideband, cells, readouts, bars and the conclusion labels were written into the plan, dated, before the governed result existed, with the exceptions the record names; lockstep's findings log was not registered in advance. A pinned cell is the one cell a decision was tied to before the run.
Regime
The exact conditions a number was measured under: models, quantization, block length, prompts, seeds, label space. Every finding states its regime on its own line.
Source line
Every sideband finding lists the committed files its numbers come from, and sideband's lint refuses a finding without one.
Erratum, dated note, amendment
Corrections are appended beside the text they correct, with a date, and the original is never rewritten. The timeline on this page collects them.
Mixture of experts, router
A model whose layers hold many small expert networks and send each token to a few of them. The router's choices are addresses, and at S3 they are the whole stream.
NF4
A weight format of four bits per weight, used to fit the target model on a single consumer graphics card. It is the primary regime of sideband's runs on the real models.
h_a, reconstruction
The target's internal state at the anchor, and the attempt to rebuild it from the stream with a linear map. The erratum showed the state follows from the free next token distribution.
Seed noise floor
How far the same decoder's tokens per check moves when only the random seed changes. It says what a conversion bar is worth at a given prompt count.
Fresh history export, withheld
The public repository holds the records' files as they stood, without the private commit history. What a folder leaves out is listed in its WITHHELD.md and marked in place.
Byte exact, the fp32 contract (amber)
An archive decodes byte for byte when the draft alone reproduces the stored text to the last byte. amber showed that holds under single precision arithmetic on one box and fails for decoders whose precision differs from the encoder's.
Rank corrections, bits per token (amber)
amber stores, for each token, where the true token sat in the draft's ranked guesses. The archive's size per token is the compression figure, measured against general purpose compressors.
Range coder, conformance vectors (lockstep)
A pure integer entropy coder, with fixed test inputs and their exact byte outputs, so another implementation can show it matches on what those vectors cover.
Pack rate, break-even (lockstep)
How fast an archive can be produced, against the rate at which the cost model says archiving would pay for itself. The measured rate fell far short.

The plain words explanations, the section intros and the reading guide are this page's own wording, not the record's; they add no claim and carry no chip.

A0 / A1 (Stage-A ladder)
The two user-launched rungs of the OQ-16 actuator stage (docs/BLUEPRINT.md STAGE-A FREEZE, 2026-09-05).
ABSORBED
The strong-null label of the full-free re-tests (F-24, F-25, F-28), one of the mechanical labels per (source, label space) read from both harvests of a source: the gate D = acc(fplus_b) - acc(fplus_n) (F-25 and F-28: acc(fplus_r), the corrected twin) at the off2 gate cell (the registered reader mlp256 in F-24, F-25 and F-28's lens stage; each stronger reader in F-28's reader stages) fires on neither harvest under the Phase-3 triple rule (CI95 lower > 0, all three seeds positive, point >= +0.005) AND every D row's CI95 upper is below +0.005 on both harvests (F-24's registered rule; its siblings READER-INVALID / SURVIVES-FULL-FREE / SPLIT / NOT-SHOWN / INCOMPLETE).
Acceptance probability, per-position (1 - TV(p, q))
Under the Leviathan rejection scheme the probability that a drafted token is accepted equals one minus the total-variation distance between the target's p and the (possibly steered) draft's q at that position.
Actuator
The mechanism that turns a signal into acceptance: bucket-bias steering (D-8/D-10; dead as built on real models at every tested k, F-12/F-13/F-14; the fine-k oracle cell is unrun), adaptive block scheduling on probes (K-3 Attempt #1, F-19), the outcome-head scheduler (OQ-15, F-21), and SD^2-style hidden-state injection (OQ-16 Stage A, frozen, unrun).
ACTUATOR-CEILING NULL
The Stage-A A0 outcome if oracle_h (the TRUE h_a injected) fails to beat vanilla by the E6 bar: "not even the TRUE anchor state converts through this frozen-drafter rank-512 actuator at this training budget on this box" (docs/BLUEPRINT.md STAGE-A FREEZE).
ADDRESS-BEYOND-TOKEN
F-26's prefetch reading on the OLMoE S3 harvests: HISTORY_TOK minus FREE_TOK at horizon j = 1, in hit rate at residency budget B = 16 of the 64 experts per gate; SIGNAL iff the paired CI95 lower > 0 and the point >= +0.05 (the pinned floor), NEGATIVE iff the CI95 upper < 0, else NOT-SHOWN; "the sideband sentence follows ADDRESS-BEYOND-TOKEN alone".
Alignment-destroyed
A feature block taken from a different block of the same prompt (slots of anchor (a+17) mod row-length, F-16), so per-prompt statistics survive but the block-aligned signal is gone.
Analytic acceptance (D-9 headline)
E[1 - TV(p_j, q_j)] recorded at every DRAFTED position: unconditional, variance-reduced, exogenous to accept/reject outcomes.
Analytic vs empirical vs probe units
Three layers the findings never convert between: probe top-1 accuracy margins (K-2), per-position analytic acceptance and tokens per call (K-3), and wall-clock (K-5, unmeasured).
Anchor-state identity (F-23)
The 2026-09-05 dated erratum's fact: h_a, the verifier's final-norm output at the anchor, is the lm_head input, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's lm_head is an untied, bias-free linear map of full column rank (128256 x 4096), so the anchor's logits determine h_a exactly and the full next-token distribution p determines it up to floating-point error (results/real/oq16_ha_identity.json: full-logit inverse relative error 2.5e-8 median; top-4097 log-prob recovery per-row R^2 +1.0000; top-32 R^2 +0.014 on 48 pinned rows).

Beta
Steering strength, the magnitude of the additive log-space bias.
Binding gate

Block
One draft-then-verify round: gamma drafted tokens, one target verify forward, emitted = accepted + 1 (the bonus or residual token).
Block-aligned information vs prompt-level statistics
The distinction the stale-target control draws: information tied to THIS block's anchor (what steering needs) versus information about the prompt overall (which a shifted twin also carries).
Block-start alignment
The load-bearing harvest convention (sideband.harvest.label_index): the label at offset j is the bucket of token x_{a+j-1}; offset 1 is the token sampled from p_a itself; K-2 reads offsets >= 2.
Bonus token / residual token
The one token the verifier always emits after a block: sampled from the residual distribution on a rejection, or from the target's distribution at position gamma+1 when every draft is accepted.
Break-even hint budget

Stale-target control (DI-1; five-seed)
Replace every (run, prompt)'s targets with a seeded permutation of its own block rows so NO block-aligned information exists for any arm, re-select lambda inside the control, and re-measure the TEL-vs-NOISE margin; if the live margin is within the spread of such zero-information draws, existence is unsupported.
Staleness / persistence / run length / hold rule
F-4 (synthetic): a block-held bucket prediction pays only when the expected concept run length comfortably exceeds gamma (rho=0.6, run length 2.5, was net-harmful at every offset even for a perfect decoder).
Standardization pathology
F-22's critic finding: Standardizer.from_moments (src/sideband/outcome_head.py) floors only exactly-zero sd, rare mass512 buckets carry TRAIN sd down to about 1e-7, and two out-of-support oq15 TEST prompts (orig 140, 37) produced standardized inputs of 26,662 and 14,082, making every trained arm 4-8x worse than the mean predictor on those clusters and inflating the registered deltas by about 25% (the unregistered drop-two diagnostic gives delta_noise +0.1327 vs the registered +0.176469).

A proposed track: store a spec-decode trace (accept bits plus corrections entropy-coded under the draft) so text decompresses with the draft alone, with the rejection rate as attestation of the decoder.
Verify call / target call
One forward of the target model over a block.
Void semantics / void-rerun
What happens when the pairwise budget guard breaches at verdict: gate metrics are QUARANTINED unread (budget columns only consulted), ONE re-freeze retunes toward lower budget, the stage reruns at the reserved seed; a second breach FAILS that leg.
