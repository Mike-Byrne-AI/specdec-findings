Synthetic world (F-0 .. F-9)

The full summary

The first arc built the verifier and a synthetic concept-HMM testbed (tokens grouped into ground-truth concept buckets, with a tunable draft) and used it to fix the shapes that every later experiment inherits. The verifier is exact by enumeration and at float64 epsilon under measurement (F-0, F-1), so acceptance rate is the only outcome any steering experiment can move. In this world most rejections are cross-bucket (76.1% to 89.3%, F-2), always-on bucket boosting self-cancels unless gated on disagreement (F-3), a block-held prediction ages out unless concept persistence comfortably exceeds gamma (F-4), a narrow sideband with a weak decoder is harmful ungated and harmless gated (F-5), a centered posterior bias dominates argmax boosting (F-7), and dense per-position hints lose 20:1 in a bit ledger (F-9). The structural result is F-6: steering from free information and from a stale telemetry oracle are identical curves here, so this world can never show a telemetry win and the existence question had to move to real models.
